🚘 Ed’s Driving Theory Test App

Free, offline, and ad-free app to help UK learner drivers prepare for their theory test
Created by a dad and his son — built for learners, not profit.

https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.james.murrell.edstheorytest

✨ Features

📝 Practice 200+ DVSA-style theory questions
✅ Clear explanations for every answer
🚦 Road sign gallery with images & descriptions
🎯 Category-specific practice or full mock tests
📊 Track your progress (streaks, scores, history – coming soon!)
🔒 No ads, no subscriptions, no data collection
📱 Works completely offline

📖 The Story

When Ed started revising, we tried the usual apps…
❌ Too many ads
❌ Locked content behind subscriptions
❌ Confusing navigation
❌ Data collection we didn’t want

So one weekend, with Ed at my side, we opened Android Studio and built our own.
Now thousands of learners across the UK can revise without distractions. 🎉
